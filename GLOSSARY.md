# Castle Solutions

Public web landing page and presence for Castle Solutions, a consultancy and technology engineering firm specializing in software architecture, cloud platforms, and applied artificial intelligence solutions.

## Language

**Castle Solutions**:
The technology and engineering brand providing software, cloud, and applied artificial intelligence solutions.
_Avoid_: Castle, The Agency

**Landing Page**:
The public single-page entry point representing Castle Solutions at `castlesolutions.com.co`.
_Avoid_: Website, Portal, Home

**Canonical Domain**:
The primary apex web address (`castlesolutions.com.co`) used for all public traffic and canonical SEO indexing.
_Avoid_: Base URL, Web address
